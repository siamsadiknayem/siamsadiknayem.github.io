Since July 2026 I have been teaching two connected courses as an Adjunct Lecturer in the Department of EEE at Southeast University: **EEE 237 Continuous Signals and Linear Systems** and **EEE 325 Digital Signal Processing I**, along with the DSP laboratory (EEE 326).

The lecture materials for both courses are now collected on this site:

- [EEE 237 Continuous Signals and Linear Systems]({{ '/courses/eee237/' | relative_url }}) — signals and systems, LTI system properties, impulse response and convolution, with course notes for further reading.
- [EEE 325 Digital Signal Processing I]({{ '/courses/eee325/' | relative_url }}) — from sinusoids and phasors through spectrum, Fourier series, sampling and aliasing, to FIR filters and frequency response.

Each lecture opens in Google Drive in a new tab, and can be downloaded. The pages update automatically when new material is added to the course folders.

The slides draw on excellent openly shared teaching material — *DSP First* by McClellan and Schafer, and lecture notes from Princeton, Purdue and Cambridge. The authors are credited on each course page.
