**Siam Sadik Nayem** is a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University and an Adjunct Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Southeast University, both in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

He received his B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) in 2025, and received the Dean's Award in every year of the programme.

His research focuses on biomedical optical sensing. In his undergraduate thesis he developed a non-invasive glucose measurement system that integrates an optical sensor with Vis-NIR spectroscopy and optimised machine learning. This work was presented as a first-author paper at the 2025 IEEE International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence and Networking (QPAIN) and contributed to a peer-reviewed article in *ACS Omega* (2026). He has also co-authored work on an IoT-enabled UAV platform for patient health monitoring. His broader interests include low-cost and wearable optical sensors and machine learning for physiological signals and clinical screening.

At Bangladesh University and Southeast University he teaches courses including Continuous Signals and Linear Systems, Digital Signal Processing I, Electronic Devices and Circuits, and Microprocessor and Assembly Language.

As a student he was Chair of the IEEE Student Branch at KUET, where he led the organising committee of ProCon 2024, and the founding Chair of the IEEE Computer Society KUET Student Branch Chapter. He led the autonomous systems team of KUET's Mars rover team, Team Durbar, at the International Rover Challenge 2024.

He is currently seeking PhD opportunities in biomedical optics, optical sensing and machine learning for health monitoring.
